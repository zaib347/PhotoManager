How it works

Three steps, and the first one is the only work you do.

  1. 1

    Add your folders

    Point it at the folders or whole drives that hold your photos. Before the first scan you choose what to skip: tiny icons and stickers, screenshots, short clips, or whole folders.

  2. 2

    It reads everything, in the background

    Dates, cameras and places come from each photo itself (EXIF, file names, Google and iCloud exports). Copies of the same photo are spotted on the way. You can browse while it works.

  3. 3

    Browse years in seconds

    One timeline, day by day, with your days grouped into events and trips. Unplug a drive and its photos wait for it; plug it back in, even as another drive letter, and they're back.

Clean up, safely

Free up gigabytes without ever losing a photo.

Duplicates finds exact copies across all your drives, even with different names. Similar photos groups bursts and near-identical shots and suggests the best one to keep.

Nothing is deleted outright. Removed photos wait in a hidden folder on the same drive, and Recently removed puts them back with one click. The Recycle Bin only gets them when you say so.

  • Organize files into folders by date, with a preview first
  • Rename by pattern: date, place, counter
  • Every move and rename is journaled and can be undone

Private by design

Your photos never leave your computer.

  • Files stay where they are. PhotoManager reads your folders; it doesn't copy them into a vault.
  • Originals stay original. Edits, rotations and dates are kept in the library, not burned into files.
  • Every change can be undone. Moves, renames and removals are journaled first.
  • AI stays on this PC. Models download only when you ask, and faces are never uploaded.

Questions

Good to know

Does PhotoManager upload my photos?

No. It works entirely on your computer. Even the optional AI features run locally; their models are downloaded once, only if you install them.

Will it move or change my files?

Not unless you ask. Browsing, rating, tagging, editing and rotating never touch your files. When you do organize, rename or remove photos, you see a preview first, and every change is journaled so it can be undone.

What happens when I unplug a drive?

Its photos stay in your timeline and are marked offline. Plug it back in, even if Windows gives it a different letter, and they're available again. Nothing is marked missing just because a drive is away.

Do I need the AI features?

No. Everything else works without them, and they're off by default. Each one is a separate download you can remove at any time, which deletes everything it made.

Which photos and videos does it read?

JPEG, HEIC, PNG, WebP, GIF, AVIF and TIFF; RAW files from most cameras (DNG, CR2, CR3, NEF, ARW, ORF, RW2, RAF, SRW, PEF); and MP4, MOV, MKV, AVI, 3GP, MTS and WebM videos.

Mac or Linux?

Windows comes first. macOS and Linux versions are planned once the Windows version is settled.

What will it cost?

Pricing for the full release will be announced before it ships, and there will be a free trial.
